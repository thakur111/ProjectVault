Why NEET Rank Calculations Matter

NEET UG results demonstrate massive swings in score-vs-rank outcomes from year to year. In 2024, unprecedented rank inflation was observed, with scores of 650 resulting in ranks around 26,000, whereas in 2023 a similar score yielded a rank of 7,000. These swings completely modify college cutoffs.

This dashboard is built to provide transparency. By modeling both the 2024 hyper-inflation curve and the 2023 moderate-inflation curve, you can plan conservative and optimistic admission targets safely.

Counselling Stages & Rounds

Round 1 (July - August)
Registration & Choice Filling
First stage of AIQ and State counselling. Candidates submit choices. Seat allotment is published. Upgradable choice is allowed in subsequent rounds.
Round 2 (August - September)
Upgraded Allocations
Mandatory locking of choices for vacant seats. Candidates who selected upgrade are re-allotted seats based on rank priority. Free exit policies expire.
Round 3 / Mop-up Round (September)
Mop-Up Allocations
Residual seats from Round 2 are compiled. Fresh choice locking is executed. If a seat is allocated, resignation is not allowed under normal circumstances.
Stray Vacancy Round (October)
Final Stray Allocations
Conducted online or offline to fill remaining vacancy slots. Strict parameters are applied. No fresh registrations are typically allowed.

Counselling FAQs

What is the difference between AIQ and State Quota counselling?
All India Quota (AIQ) covers 15% of MBBS/BDS seats in all state-run government medical colleges across India and is open to all candidates. Respective State Counselling bodies manage 85% of government seats and 100% of private college seats inside their boundaries, reserved for candidates holding the state's domicile certificate.
What does "Free Exit" mean in NEET Counselling?
Free Exit is a provision available only in Round 1 of counselling. If you are allotted a seat in Round 1 and choose not to report or join the college, you do not face any penalties, and your registration security deposit is not forfeited. This exit becomes restricted in subsequent rounds.
How does the upgrade system work?
If you get a college allotted in Round 1, you can report to the college and express choice willingness to "Upgrade" in Round 2. When choices are locked for Round 2, the counselling engine will attempt to allocate a higher-priority college from your list. If upgraded, your Round 1 seat is automatically assigned to someone else.
